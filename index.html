Currently Working On:

Argo Boattail Design & Manufacturing Development

I am working alongside a team designing a boattail to create a gradual transition between Argo’s 8-inch rocket body and 6.5-inch aft section, with the goal of reducing aerodynamic drag to support the team’s 100,000-foot altitude target. I am responsible for manufacturing ocnsiderations and construction, beginning with a preliminary SOLIDWORKS model and an evaluation of metal additive manufacturing methods, including LPBF/DMLS. After identifying cost limitations, I shifted focus to formed sheet metal and carbon-fiber fabrication. I am currently calculating structural loads to assess sheet-metal feasibility and determine an appropriate thickness, alongside researching carbon-fiber layering over a 3D-printed model.
